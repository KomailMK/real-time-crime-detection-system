TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

React Warning on Login:
  Status: ✅ FIXED
  Solution: useEffect hook in auth/page.tsx line 38-43

Webcam Not Working:
  Causes:
    • Missing browser permissions
    • USB camera not detected
    • Browser doesn't support getUserMedia
  Solutions:
    • Grant camera permission in browser settings
    • Check USB camera in Device Manager
    • Use modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

IP Camera Connection Issues:
  See: IP_CAMERA_INTEGRATION.md → Troubleshooting section

YOLO Not Detecting Objects:
